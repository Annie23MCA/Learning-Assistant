AI-Enhanced Learning Assistant Platform using Natural Language Processing

ABOUT



Subjective paper evaluation is a complex and labor-intensive task when performed manually. A major challenge in automating this process using Artificial Intelligence (AI) lies in the insufficient understanding and acceptance of data. Various attempts have been made to assess students' answers using computational methods, but most rely on traditional word counts or specific keyword matching, which limits accuracy. Additionally, the availability of well-curated datasets for subjective evaluation remains a challenge. This paper presents a novel approach that integrates machine learning and natural language processing (NLP) techniques to automate the evaluation of descriptive answers. It leverages tools such as WordNet, Word2Vec, Word Mover's Distance (WMD), cosine similarity, multinomial Naïve Bayes (MNB), and term frequency-inverse document frequency (TF-IDF). Solution statements and extracted keywords are used to assess responses, while a machine learning model is trained to predict answer grades. Experimental results demonstrate that WMD outperforms cosine similarity in evaluating subjective answers. With sufficient training, the proposed model can function as a standalone grading system. The experiments achieved an accuracy of 88% without the MNB model, and incorporating MNB further reduced the error rate by 1.3%.
